Professional Approach

My work focuses on market analysis, price formation, risk assessment, and regulatory change; I have also worked on fiscal policy problems. To do so, I combine data, econometrics, mathematical and economic modeling, and scenario simulation. I aim to turn analysis into clear conclusions that are useful for decision-making. Throughout my career I have worked with technical, commercial, financial, legal, and regulatory teams, and have collaborated with companies and academic teams on market analysis and applied research projects.

Research & Teaching

Energy markets and price formation

Energy economics · Industrial organization
  • 2021
    Strategic behaviors and transfer of wholesale costs to retail prices in the electricity market: Evidence from Colombia.
    Correa-Giraldo, M., García-Rendón, J. J., & Pérez, A.
    Energy Economics · DOI: 10.1016/j.eneco.2021.105276

    Key contribution: Pass-through of wholesale costs to residential tariffs is more than complete (115% on average), and statistically larger for dominant retailers integrated with generation, suggesting a possible exercise of market power.

    View article →
  • 2024
    Efecto de la entrada en operación de la central hidroeléctrica más grande y de las energías renovables no convencionales en Colombia sobre el precio de bolsa. [Effect of the start of operation of Colombia's largest hydroelectric plant and of non-conventional renewable energies on the spot price.]
    García-Rendón, J. J., Correa-Giraldo, M., & Gutiérrez-Gómez, A.
    Cuadernos de Economía · in Spanish · DOI: 10.15446/cuad.econ.v43n91.103081

    Key contribution: Solar and wind reduce the spot price by about 12% through the merit-order effect, while higher demand and the first Hidroituango turbine can raise it.

    View article →
  • 2023
    Energías renovables no convencionales y efecto orden de mérito en el mercado eléctrico colombiano. [Non-conventional renewable energies and the merit-order effect in the Colombian electricity market.]
    García-Rendón, J. J., Correa-Giraldo, M., & Pérez, A.
    Book chapter · Editorial Universidad Nacional de Colombia · in Spanish

    Key contribution: The size of the merit-order effect of renewables depends closely on the system's hydro supply.
